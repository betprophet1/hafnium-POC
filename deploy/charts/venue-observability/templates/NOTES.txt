The venue's dashboards (folders System and Business), data sources and alert rules are installed.

Grafana is not exposed. To look at it from this machine:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get secret grafana -o jsonpath='{.data.admin-password}' | base64 -d; echo
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/grafana 13000:80      # then http://127.0.0.1:13000, user admin
